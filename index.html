        Get the app

        Opens full screen and works offline at the field.

        On iPhone or iPad (Safari)

        1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
        2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
        3. Tap Add.

        Or use the installer file

        Tap the button below, then open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded near the top, and tap Install. The file only adds the app icon.

        Download installer

        On Android (Chrome)

        Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

        On a computer (Chrome or Edge)

        Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

        The app keeps its own copy of your tournament. To move one you started in the browser, use Menu → Download backup there, then Menu → Restore from backup in the app.
